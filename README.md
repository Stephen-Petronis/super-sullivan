# Super Sullivan

A party in four games, starring Sullivan. Part of the Monster Pizza arcade.

1. **Blow Out the Candles** – hold and swipe across an 11-candle, two-tier cake before they relight.
2. **Cake Goblins** – pop the icing goblins that burst out of the cake; big ones split.
3. **Dirt Bike Trail** – hold gas, tilt (or arrows) to land; gas in the air on big ramps for backflips.
4. **Rec Center Hockey** – NES-style 3-on-3 for one minute. Hold SHOOT to power up, double-tap for a slap shot.

Each game has a top-5 high score board with arcade initials (saved on the device).

`index.html` is fully self-contained (art, music and code built in). Tuning values live in the `CFG` block at the top of the script. Source art and the theme song are in `art/`.
